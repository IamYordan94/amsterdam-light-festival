12 works · images: AI illustrations
01AmstelOn the water

Northern Whale

Ada Lindqvist — Sweden

A wireframe whale breaches the black water of the Amstel, drawn in a single unbroken line of warm light. From a passing boat the animal appears to swim alongside you.

Amstel, near Blauwbrug

02Herengracht

Rituals of Repetition

Studio Verval — Netherlands

Hundreds of illuminated rings stack into a facade-sized wall of circles, doubled perfectly by the canal below. Every ring pulses a half-second after its neighbour.

Herengracht 502

03OosterdokOn the water

Polaris

Kees van Dijk — Netherlands

A six-metre star hovers above the water and finds its twin in the reflection — a compass point for the whole route, visible from three bridges away.

Oosterdok, opposite NEMO

04Amstel

Ovum

Marta Wroblewska — Poland

A woven lattice egg, four metres tall, breathing between coral and gold. Sit inside the light and the canal noise drops away.

Amstel, Kloveniersburgwal corner

05OosterdokOn the water

Vortex

Collective HALO — United Kingdom

Suspended between two cranes, a spinning disc of violet light throws a slow spiral across the harbour — the largest work of the 2026 edition.

Oosterdokskade, above the water

06PrinsengrachtOn the water

Cut Diamond

Noor Haddad — Lebanon

A floating gem of blue neon edges, cut from nothing but line and reflection. Best seen low, from a boat deck, when the water is still.

Prinsengracht 263, at the water

07PrinsengrachtOn the water

Wave Function

Bureau Tide — Netherlands

A sine wave of 2,400 LEDs rides across the bridge and keeps travelling in the canal below. Boats sail straight through the crest.

Prinsengracht, Berenstraat bridge

08KeizersgrachtOn the water

Second Wave

Bureau Tide — Netherlands

The companion piece to Wave Function, tuned a semitone lower and coloured in dawn pink. Together they mark the western turn of the route.

Keizersgracht, Leidsegracht corner

09AmstelOn the water

Bloom

Sanne de Wit — Netherlands

Nine floating flowers open and close on the river's surface, timed to the wake of passing boats. Sail slowly and they follow you.

Amstel, opposite Carré

10HerengrachtOn the water

Firework Bridge

Atelier Lumen — France

An arch of white sparks frozen mid-explosion over the water — a firework that never falls, mirrored into a full circle by the canal.

Herengracht, Brouwersgracht crossing

11OosterdokOn the water

Amber Gate

Tomás Ferreira — Portugal

A monumental gate of amber light drifts on a pontoon, changing shape as your boat passes through it.

Oosterdok, floating platform

12OosterdokOn the water

Night Tower

Lieke Bos — Netherlands

The 16th-century tower is re-lit hour by hour in the colours of the canal water below — the closing work of the 2026 route.

Montelbaanstoren, Oudeschans

Best seen from a boat
