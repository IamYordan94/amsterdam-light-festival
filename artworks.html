01AmstelOn the water

Northern Whale

Light sculpture on the water

A wireframe whale breaches the black water of the Amstel, drawn in a single unbroken line of warm light. From a passing boat the animal appears to swim alongside you.

Amstel, near Blauwbrug

02Herengracht

Rituals of Repetition

Concentric light rings on the water

Hundreds of illuminated rings stack into a facade-sized wall of circles, doubled perfectly by the canal below. Every ring pulses a half-second after its neighbour.

Herengracht 502

03OosterdokOn the water

Polaris

Navigational light work on the water

A six-metre star hovers above the water and finds its twin in the reflection — a compass point for the whole route, visible from three bridges away.

Oosterdok, opposite NEMO

04Amstel

Ovum

Glowing form on the water

A woven lattice egg, four metres tall, breathing between coral and gold. Sit inside the light and the canal noise drops away.

Amstel, Kloveniersburgwal corner

05OosterdokOn the water

Vortex

Spiral light work over the water

Suspended between two cranes, a spinning disc of violet light throws a slow spiral across the harbour — the largest work of the 2026 edition.

Oosterdokskade, above the water

06PrinsengrachtOn the water

Cut Diamond

Faceted light sculpture on the quay

A floating gem of blue neon edges, cut from nothing but line and reflection. Best seen low, from a boat deck, when the water is still.

Prinsengracht 263, at the water

07PrinsengrachtOn the water

Wave Function

Light work on the bridge

A sine wave of 2,400 LEDs rides across the bridge and keeps travelling in the canal below. Boats sail straight through the crest.

Prinsengracht, Berenstraat bridge

08KeizersgrachtOn the water

Second Wave

Low light ribbon across the water

The companion piece to Wave Function, tuned a semitone lower and coloured in dawn pink. Together they mark the western turn of the route.

Keizersgracht, Leidsegracht corner

09AmstelOn the water

Bloom

Flower-shaped light work on the quay

Nine floating flowers open and close on the river's surface, timed to the wake of passing boats. Sail slowly and they follow you.

Amstel, opposite Carré

10HerengrachtOn the water

Firework Bridge

Bridge-wide light installation

An arch of white sparks frozen mid-explosion over the water — a firework that never falls, mirrored into a full circle by the canal.

Herengracht, Brouwersgracht crossing

11OosterdokOn the water

Amber Gate

Illuminated arch over the water

A monumental gate of amber light drifts on a pontoon, changing shape as your boat passes through it.

Oosterdok, floating platform

12OosterdokOn the water

Night Tower

Vertical light work on the water

The 16th-century tower is re-lit hour by hour in the colours of the canal water below — the closing work of the 2026 route.

Montelbaanstoren, Oudeschans

13HerengrachtOn the water

Lantern Herd

Drifting lantern light on the water

A herd of small lantern-creatures of warm light drifts in a loose line along the Herengracht, travelling with the current. From the bank they read as a flock; from a boat you pass through them.

Herengracht, near Leidsestraat

14OosterdokOn the water

Signal

Vertical light beam from the water

A single thin beam of ice-blue light rises straight out of the black water, high enough to be seen across the whole Oosterdok. Its base is a low pontoon, lit from below.

Oosterdok, near the maritime museum

15KeizersgrachtOver the water

Paper Crane

Suspended light work between the quays

An origami crane folded out of white light hangs on fine cables between two quay walls, its folds catching the light differently from every angle. Passing under it is the point.

Keizersgracht, near Westermarkt

16PrinsengrachtOn the canal wall

Tide

Bands of light along the canal wall

Bands of mint light run along a long stretch of brick wall just above the waterline, compressing and repeating into the distance. Slow enough to look like a tide moving in.

Prinsengracht, near Noorderkerk

17AmstelOver the water

Moths

Swarm of small lights above the water

Dozens of small pale lights drift in the air above the Amstel like moths at a lamp, each on its own slow path. Stand still and they gather; walk on and they scatter.

Amstel, near the Stopera

18HerengrachtOn the water

Stairs

Staircase of light rising from the water

A wide staircase of pale amber light rises out of the canal, each step a slab of glow. The flight is doubled in the still water underneath it, so it appears to descend too.

Herengracht, near the Brouwersgracht

19KeizersgrachtOver the water

Woven Water

Light net suspended between bridges

A broad net of thin white light is stretched high between two bridges. Seen from a boat underneath, the mesh hangs against the sky and fills the canal below with its reflection.

Keizersgracht, between two bridges

20OosterdokOn the water

Afterglow

Low field of ambient light on the water

No single object: a wide low field of warm light lies across the water and spills onto the quay stone, brightest at the far edge. The quietest work on the route, and the last stop.

Oosterdok, near the library

Best seen from a boat
